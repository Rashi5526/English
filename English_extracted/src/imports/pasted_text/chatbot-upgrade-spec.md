# 07 — GRAMMAR CORRECTION MUST BE EXTREMELY IMPORTANT

The learner specifically wants to LEARN from their mistakes.

For every important correction, explain briefly:

### ❌ "I was go"

### ✅ "I went"

**Why?**

"Was go" is incorrect because "go" needs to be changed to its past form here.

---

### ❌ "I see my friend"

### ✅ "I saw my friend"

**Why?**

You're talking about yesterday → past tense.

---

### ❌ "we was"

### ✅ "we were"

**Why?**

"We" uses **were**, not "was."

---

Do NOT overwhelm the user with huge grammar lectures.

Keep explanations:

**short + clear + memorable.**

# 08 — SENTENCE STRUCTURE IS A CORE FEATURE

The chatbot must specifically teach:

## HOW ENGLISH WORDS ARE ARRANGED.

This is extremely important.

Many learners know vocabulary but don't know how to arrange those words naturally.

Example:

User:

> "Yesterday market I went."

AI:

### Better:

> "I went to the market yesterday."

Then explain:

**Basic English sentence structure:**

**Subject + Verb + Object + Time**

> I + went + to the market + yesterday.

Teach sentence construction naturally through the user's own mistakes.
